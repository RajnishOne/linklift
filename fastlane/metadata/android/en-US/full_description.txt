LinkLift is a fast, lightweight, and private YouTube and Instagram video downloader for Android. Easily download YouTube videos, Shorts, Instagram Reels, posts, and audio in high-definition (HD, 1080p, 2K, 4K) directly to your device.

Save your favorite content for offline viewing, extract MP3 audio from music and podcasts, and download Instagram Reels with crystal-clear quality — all with zero ads, zero trackers, and complete privacy.

Key Features:

▶️ YouTube Video & Audio Downloader
• Download YouTube videos in multiple resolutions: 720p, 1080p Full HD, 2K, and 4K
• Fast YouTube Shorts downloader with instant preview
• Convert & extract YouTube videos to high-quality audio (MP3, M4A)
• Batch download full YouTube playlists and channels
• Local video and audio stream merging via native MediaMuxer for seamless playback

📸 Instagram Video & Reels Downloader
• Download Instagram Reels, videos, photos, and multi-slide carousels
• Save high-resolution Instagram media directly to your device gallery
• Instant share: Send links directly from Instagram to LinkLift via the system share sheet

🎬 Built-In Fullscreen Media Player
• Play downloaded videos and audio files offline without leaving the app
• Gesture controls for brightness, volume, and seeking
• Playback speed controls (0.5x to 2x) and video quality selector

🔒 100% Private, Ad-Free & Open Source
• Zero ads, zero trackers, and zero telemetry
• Powered by an on-device Python engine (yt-dlp and Instaloader via Chaquopy)
• Completely offline processing with no cloud intermediaries or account required

How to Use LinkLift:
1. Tap "Share" on any YouTube video, Short, or Instagram Reel, and choose LinkLift (or copy and paste the link).
2. Preview the media and pick your preferred video resolution or audio format.
3. Tap Download to save it directly to your device!
