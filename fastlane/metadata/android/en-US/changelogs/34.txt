- Large playlist support: Expanded batch extraction limit up to 1000 items to fetch and download full 400+ video playlists
- One-tap retry: Added "Retry all failed" and selection retry buttons in Downloads screen to effortlessly restart failed tasks
- Queue priority: Retried tasks are prioritized to jump straight to the head of the download queue
- File conflict policy: Added a new setting to Skip, Overwrite, or Add serial suffix to existing files (ideal for resuming partial playlist downloads)
- Subtitles & lyrics: Added support for downloading and auto-converting captions to .srt alongside media files for VLC and other media players
